**Mandate:** Whenever the user asks for an explanation of how a system, feature, or logic works, you MUST NOT rely on your general knowledge or assumptions. 
**Action:** You MUST use `view_file` or `grep_search` to read the actual source code of the relevant component first.
**Goal:** Ensure that all answers are 100% accurate, definitive, and not "floating" or vague. Your explanations must reflect the exact current implementation in the codebase.
